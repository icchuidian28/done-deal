QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a repair cost?

Repair pricing is upon inspection of the motorcycle. The condition, issue, parts required, and labor involved can vary, so we inspect the unit before giving a service price.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so we can manage inspection time and availability. You can contact Done Deal using the booking form below.

Can you diagnose a problem I can't identify?

Yes. Tell us the symptoms—such as hard starting, unusual noise, poor acceleration, vibration, overheating, or electrical problems—and we'll inspect the unit.

Do you replace parts?

Parts may be replaced when inspection shows they are needed. We'll discuss the recommended replacement with you before proceeding.
